# Motion aftereffect: which display moves more coherently downward?

Thank you for taking part. Please read this before you start.

**Keep your eyes on the black cross in the middle of the screen the whole time.** Two
round patches of black dots sit either side of it. Do not look at the patches directly.

**Adapting.** At first, and again between test displays, the dots in the two patches move
for a while: in the main session the dots on the left all drift upward and the dots on
the right jitter in every direction. The first period lasts a minute; the later ones ten
seconds. Just keep looking at the cross.

**Testing.** Then both patches change to new dots for two seconds. In each, some dots may
drift downward or upward together while the rest jitter. After the dots disappear, say
which patch looked as if it was moving **more coherently downward** overall:

- **Left arrow**: the left patch looked more coherently downward.
- **Right arrow**: the right patch looked more coherently downward.

Go with your impression; there is no right answer. If you are not sure, guess. If you do
not answer within five seconds, that test is repeated later. After your answer the
adapting dots come back and the next test follows.

The session lasts about 45 minutes. Tell the experimenter if you need a break: a pause of more than half a minute lets the
adaptation fade, so the minute of adapting is repeated afterwards.

Press **SPACE** to start.
