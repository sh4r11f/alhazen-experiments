In this experiment you will remember short lists of digits.

On each trial, one to six different digits appear one at a time in the middle of the
screen. Remember them, in order. A short pause follows, then a + appears as a warning,
and then one more digit: the test digit.

Decide as quickly as you can whether the test digit was in the list you just saw.

  {POSITIVE_KEY}  =  yes, it was in the list
  {NEGATIVE_KEY}  =  no, it was not

Respond as quickly as possible while making very few mistakes. A light below the digits
tells you at once whether your answer was right: green for right, red for wrong.

Then type the whole list back, in the order it was shown, and press ENTER. BACKSPACE
deletes a digit.

The first trials are practice.

Press SPACE to begin.
