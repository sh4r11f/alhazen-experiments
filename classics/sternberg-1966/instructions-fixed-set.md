In this experiment you will remember a small set of digits for a whole part of the
session.

At the start of each part your digits are shown on the screen. Learn them, then press
SPACE. On every trial of that part a + appears as a warning, then a single test digit,
which can be any digit from 0 to 9.

Decide as quickly as you can whether the test digit is one of your digits.

  {POSITIVE_KEY}  =  yes, it is one of my digits
  {NEGATIVE_KEY}  =  no, it is not

Respond as quickly as possible while making very few mistakes. A light below the digit
tells you at once whether your answer was right: green for right, red for wrong.

There are three parts, each with its own digits, and each starts with practice trials.

Press SPACE to begin.
