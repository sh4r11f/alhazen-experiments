This is a demonstration of a monkey neurophysiology task. Nothing you do is
recorded as a result, and nothing is stimulated.

In each trial a small white dot appears in the middle of the screen. Look at it and
keep looking at it.

After a moment one or two dark bars appear away from the dot. Keep your eyes on the dot
while they are up; do not look at the bars. The trial ends when the bars go off.

If your eyes leave the dot, the trial stops and comes back later.

Press SPACE to begin.
