In this experiment you watch for a small white square and press the SPACE bar as soon as
you see it.

Keep your eyes on the centre of the screen the whole time. First a small dot appears
there. Then the dot is replaced by a sign: a plus sign, or an arrow pointing left or
right. A moment later a white square may appear to the left or to the right.

An arrow tells you where the square is likely to appear, but not always. The plus sign
tells you nothing. Either way, do not move your eyes towards the square: keep looking at
the centre and press SPACE the moment the square appears, wherever it is.

On some trials no square appears at all. Then do not press anything.

Do not press before the square appears. After each trial the dot turns green if you did
the right thing and red if not.

Press SPACE to begin.
