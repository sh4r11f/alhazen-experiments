A grey square area is marked by four corner marks. A pattern of stripes inside it will
switch on and off, once every two seconds.

Use the DOWN arrow key (or the scroll wheel) to make the stripes fainter and the UP
arrow key to make them stronger. Find the faintest setting at which you can still just
see the stripes coming and going, then press SPACE. You may go below and come back up
as often as you like before you press SPACE.

Keep your glasses or contact lenses on if you use them for this distance.

Press SPACE to begin.
