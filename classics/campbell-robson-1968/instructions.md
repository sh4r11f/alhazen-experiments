Look at the small dot in the middle of the grey square area.

Each trial has two short looks, one after the other. Four corner marks appear around the
square for each look. In one of the two looks a faint pattern of soft vertical stripes
is shown inside the square; in the other there is nothing but the plain grey.

After the second look, press F if the stripes were in the FIRST look, or J if they were
in the SECOND look. Often they will be too faint to see: then make your best guess. The
dot turns green after a right answer and red after a wrong one.

Keep your glasses or contact lenses on if you use them for this distance.

Press SPACE to begin.
