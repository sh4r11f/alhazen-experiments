# Instructions

What each task's subject reads before trial one. Every task shows one section below. In
all of them: keep your eyes on the black-and-white bull's-eye in the middle of the screen
the whole time. The patterns turn around it; follow them with your attention, never with
your eyes.

## equiluminance-speed

A ring of red and green stripes turns around the bull's-eye.

Turn the scroll wheel, or press the UP and DOWN arrow keys, to change the balance between
the red and the green. At one balance the stripes seem to turn most slowly, or almost to
stop. Find that point, going past it in both directions to be sure, then press SPACE.

Press SPACE to begin.

## flicker-photometry

The ring flickers between red and green.

Turn the scroll wheel, or press the UP and DOWN arrow keys, to change the balance between
the red and the green. At one balance the flicker is weakest. Find that point, going past
it in both directions to be sure, then press SPACE.

Press SPACE to begin.

## motion-null

A ring of stripes turns around the bull's-eye. It holds two patterns turning in opposite
directions: red and green stripes, and light and dark stripes.

Do not pick out any stripe. Look at the ring as a whole and judge which way it seems to
turn. Turn the scroll wheel, or press the UP and DOWN arrow keys, to make the light and
dark stripes stronger or weaker, until the ring seems to turn neither way. Then press
SPACE.

Press SPACE to begin.

## thresholds-presence

A ring of stripes turns around the bull's-eye. Sometimes red and green stripes cover it
as well.

Turn the scroll wheel, or press the UP and DOWN arrow keys, to make the stripes you are
asked about stronger or weaker, until you can only just tell that they are there. Then
press SPACE. Some settings start with the stripes clearly visible, others with them
invisible.

Press SPACE to begin.

## thresholds-direction

A ring of light and dark stripes turns around the bull's-eye. Sometimes red and green
stripes cover it as well.

Turn the scroll wheel, or press the UP and DOWN arrow keys, to make the light and dark
stripes stronger or weaker, until you can only just tell which way they turn. Then press
SPACE. Some settings start with the stripes strong, others with them invisible.

Press SPACE to begin.

## attentive-tracking-color

A ring of stripes turns around the bull's-eye: red and green stripes, with light and dark
stripes turning the other way.

At the start of each trial two black pointers appear, aimed at a pair of RED or GREEN
stripes on opposite sides of the ring, and turn with them for a second. Then the pointers
go off. Keep following those two stripes with your attention, without moving your eyes.
After a moment the pointers flash on again. Press J if they point at the stripes you were
following, F if they point at a different pair. Guess if you are not sure.

Press SPACE to begin.

## speed-match-global

The outer ring holds red and green stripes; the inner ring holds light and dark stripes
turning the other way.

Judge the overall impression of motion in each ring, without following any one stripe.
Turn the scroll wheel, or press the UP and DOWN arrow keys, to make the inner ring turn
faster or slower until both rings seem to turn at the same speed. Then press SPACE.

Press SPACE to begin.

## speed-match-tracking

The outer ring holds red and green stripes; the inner ring holds light and dark stripes
turning the other way.

Pick a pair of stripes on opposite sides of the outer ring and follow them with your
attention as they turn, to judge how fast they go. Then switch your attention to the
inner ring and follow a pair there. Turn the scroll wheel, or press the UP and DOWN arrow
keys, to make the inner ring turn faster or slower until the two rates match; switch back
and forth as often as you need. Then press SPACE.

Press SPACE to begin.
