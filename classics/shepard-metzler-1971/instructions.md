In each trial you will see two drawings, one in each of two round windows. Each drawing
shows an object made of ten cubes joined face to face.

Decide whether the two drawings show objects of the same three-dimensional shape. The
objects may be shown in different orientations: if one object could be turned to match
the other exactly, they are the same. If they could never be made to match by any turn
(for example, because one is the mirror image of the other), they are different.

- Press **J** (right hand) as soon as you see that they are the **same**.
- Press **F** (left hand) as soon as you see that they are **different**.

Respond as quickly as possible while keeping errors to a minimum.

A small dot appears between the windows half a second before each pair: it tells you the
drawings are about to appear.

Rest your right index finger on J and your left index finger on F. Press SPACE to begin.
