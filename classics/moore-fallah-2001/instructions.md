Keep your eyes on the small dot in the middle of the screen for the whole trial.

A square will appear away from the centre. Watch it without looking at it.
Other squares will flicker all over the screen: ignore them.

Sometimes the square dims very briefly. Press SPACE as soon as you see it dim.
If it does not dim, do not press anything; the trial ends by itself.

The dimming gets harder to see as you get better at it. Looking away from the
dot ends the trial, and it comes back later.

Press SPACE to begin.
