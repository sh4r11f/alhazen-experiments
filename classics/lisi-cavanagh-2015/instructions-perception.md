Keep your eyes on the small black dot in the middle of the screen for the whole trial.

A small striped patch will appear to the right and move back and forth along a straight
path. Without looking at it, judge whether its path is tilted to the left or to the right
of vertical.

Press the LEFT arrow key if the path leans to the left, and the RIGHT arrow key if it leans
to the right. The patch keeps moving until you answer, so take the time you need.

If you look away or blink before you answer, the trial starts again.

Press SPACE to begin.
