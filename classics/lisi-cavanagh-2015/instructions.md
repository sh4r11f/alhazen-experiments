Keep your eyes on the small black dot in the middle of the screen.

A small striped patch will appear to the right and move up and down. Keep looking at the
dot while it moves.

When the dot disappears, look at the patch as quickly as you can. The patch disappears when
your eyes move; that is part of the task.

If you look away or blink before the dot disappears, the trial starts again.

Press SPACE to begin.
