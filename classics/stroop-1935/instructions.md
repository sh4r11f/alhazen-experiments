## Colour naming

In each trial a small dot appears in the middle of the screen, and then a colour: a
coloured square, or a word printed in coloured ink.

Press the key for the COLOUR OF THE INK, and ignore what the word says. If the word
"red" is printed in blue ink, press the key for blue.

{KEYS}

Answer as quickly as you can. If you press a wrong key the item stays on the screen:
correct it by pressing the right key. The first few items of each new kind are practice.

Press SPACE to begin.

## Word reading

In each trial a small dot appears in the middle of the screen, and then a colour name:
a word printed in black, or in coloured ink.

Press the key for the WORD, and ignore the colour of the ink. If the word "red" is
printed in blue ink, press the key for red.

{KEYS}

Answer as quickly as you can. If you press a wrong key the word stays on the screen:
correct it by pressing the right key. The first few items of each new kind are practice.

Press SPACE to begin.
