Each trial starts with a white rectangle on a gray screen, then a plain gray screen.

Then a photograph appears and flickers: it keeps alternating with a slightly changed
version of itself, with brief gray flashes in between. Exactly one thing is different
between the two versions. Something appears and disappears, changes its colour, or
moves to another place. The change keeps repeating until you see it.

Press SPACE as soon as you see what is changing.

Then two questions:

- Where: numbered boxes appear on the picture. Press the number (1 to 4) of the box
  the change was in.
- What kind: press P if something appeared and disappeared, C if it changed colour,
  L if it moved.

If you do not see the change within a minute, the trial ends by itself. When a word
appears in the white rectangle, it may name the part of the picture that changes.

First come six practice trials, two of each kind of change.

Press SPACE to begin.
