In each trial a small yellow dot appears in the middle of the screen. Keep your eyes on it
for the whole trial.

A patch of flickering dots then appears around it for two seconds. Most dots jump about at
random, but some of them drift together, either to the RIGHT or to the LEFT. Sometimes the
drift is strong and easy to see; often it is very weak, and sometimes there is none at all.

When the dots disappear, press the RIGHT arrow if the drift was to the right and the LEFT
arrow if it was to the left. Take your time to decide (you have about two seconds), and
guess if you are not sure: every trial needs an answer.

The dot turns green after a correct answer and red after a wrong one. When there was no
drift, the colour is random.

Press SPACE to begin.
