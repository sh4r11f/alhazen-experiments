Look at the small cross in the middle of the screen. When it looks sharp and you are
ready, press SPACE.

Half a second later a card of letters (sometimes letters and numbers) flashes on and
fades out. This is a test of your ability to read letters under these conditions, not
a test of your memory.

During the flash, or while it is fading, a short bar appears beside one row: that row is
the one to report. Do not read the card until the bar tells you which row. Keep your eyes
on the cross, and be equally ready for every row: the row is chosen at random.

Type the letters of that row, left to right, then press ENTER. Fill every box: guess when
you are not sure, and guess different letters rather than repeating one. BACKSPACE
corrects a mistake. The letters C, H, P and R never appear; please do not press them,
because they are the experimenter's keys and pressing one pauses the session.

The first trials of each new delay are practice.

Press SPACE to begin.
