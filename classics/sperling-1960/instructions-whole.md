Look at the small cross in the middle of the screen. When it looks sharp and you are
ready, press SPACE.

Half a second later a card of letters (sometimes letters and numbers) flashes on and
fades out.

Type every letter on the card, row by row from the top, each row left to right, then
press ENTER. Fill every box: guess when you are not sure, and guess different letters
rather than repeating one. BACKSPACE corrects a mistake. The letters C, H, P and R never
appear; please do not press them, because they are the experimenter's keys and pressing
one pauses the session.

The first trials of each new kind of card are practice.

Press SPACE to begin.
