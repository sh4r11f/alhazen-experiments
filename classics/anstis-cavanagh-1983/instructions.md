# Minimum-motion equiluminance: instructions

Read the section for the task you are about to do. In every task, look at the small white
cross on the blue square in the middle of the screen and keep your eyes on it.

## min-motion-constant

A small striped patch appears around the cross. The stripes flicker and may seem to move
sideways, to the LEFT or to the RIGHT.

Watch for a moment, then press:

- LEFT arrow if the stripes move to the left,
- RIGHT arrow if they move to the right,
- DOWN arrow if you see no consistent motion: nothing moves, the motion is ambiguous, the
  stripes just flicker in place, or two sets of stripes seem to slide in opposite
  directions.

There are no right or wrong answers. Answer as soon as you are sure; the patch keeps
running until you press a key.

Press SPACE to begin.

## min-motion-adjust

A small striped patch appears around the cross. The stripes seem to move sideways.
Turn the scroll wheel, or press the UP and DOWN arrows, to change the brightness of the
coloured stripes. The direction of the motion tells you which way to go: keep adjusting
until the stripes seem to stop moving in either direction (they may still flicker).
Press SPACE when the motion is least.

Press SPACE to begin.

## min-flicker-adjust

A small patch appears around the cross. It flickers between two colours (a uniform disc,
or stripes that swap colours). Turn the scroll wheel, or press the UP and DOWN arrows, to
change the brightness of the coloured part until the flicker is least. Press SPACE when
the flicker is least.

Press SPACE to begin.
