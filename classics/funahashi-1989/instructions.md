In each trial a small white dot appears in the middle of the screen. Look at it and keep
looking at it.

While you look at the dot, a small white square flashes briefly somewhere around it. Do not
look at the square: keep your eyes on the dot. Remember where the square was.

After a pause of one to six seconds the dot disappears. As soon as it does, move your eyes
to where the square was, as accurately as you can. The screen is empty then: look at the
remembered place.

Looking away from the dot before it disappears ends the trial, and it will come again later.

Press SPACE to begin.
