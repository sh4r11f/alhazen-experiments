Letters will flash one after another, very fast, in the middle of the screen. Keep your
eyes on the small white dot that comes before them, and watch the place where it was.

Almost all the letters are black. In every stream one letter is WHITE. In some streams
a black X appears somewhere after the white letter; in others there is no X.

The session has two parts. Before each part a screen tells you which task it is:

- In one part, find the white letter and remember it. After the stream, type that
  letter, then press Y if you saw a black X or N if you did not.
- In the other part, ignore the white letter. After the stream, only press Y if you
  saw a black X or N if you did not.

Take your time answering: only accuracy matters, not speed. If you are not sure, give
your best guess.

You start each stream yourself by pressing SPACE when you are ready. After every 60
streams there is a rest: rest for about a minute, then press SPACE to go on. Each part
begins with 10 practice streams.

Press SPACE to begin.
