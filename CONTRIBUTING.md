# Contributing

## Publish or contribute: two different things

**Publishing to the Experiment Hub** is open to anyone. From your own copy of an
experiment (a fork in the hub's "My experiments"), Publish puts a listing on the hub under
your own name. Nobody reviews it, and it does not touch this repository.

**Contributing to this repository** is an explicit submission: a pull request that adds
`contributed/<your-handle>/<experiment>/`. It is reviewed against the checklist below
before it is merged. Your name stays on it (`docs/listing.json: authors`, the commit
author, and the hub listing's `collection: contributed`). Every later update you send is
reviewed again in the same way; merged code is never changed on your behalf without a
review you can see.

A replication of a published experiment may also be proposed for `classics/`; say so in
the pull request.

## Review checklist

1. **Methods fidelity.** `docs/methods.md` cites the original paper and follows its
   methods section. Every intended change is a row in *Deviations from the original*.
   Every value the paper does not state is a row in *Assumed, not in the paper*, flagged
   `# ASSUMED` in the task YAML and listed under `assumed` in `docs/experiment.json`.
   `docs/listing.json: status` is honest (`original-protocol`, `adaptation` or `demo`).
2. **Tests.** `uv run pytest -q` passes with no display: trial logic on alhazen's fake
   clock with scripted inputs, the params files load, the documentation matches the
   params files, and one simulated session runs end to end.
3. **Portability.** The folder is a standalone project: its own `pyproject.toml` with
   alhazen-vision pinned by a released git tag, a `uv.lock` that `uv sync --locked`
   accepts, no imports from sibling folders, no absolute paths.
4. **Licence.** Code under the repository's MIT licence. Every image, sound or other
   asset either generated by a script in the folder or shipped with its licence stated in
   `docs/listing.json: assets`. Nothing copied from a paper or a commercial set.
5. **No participant data, no private rig settings.** No recorded sessions, no subject
   identifiers, no hostnames, usernames, home paths, IP addresses of real machines, lab
   storage paths or institution-specific settings. Rig files carry example geometry only.

`python scripts/validate_catalog.py` checks 3 and 5 mechanically and parts of 1; a person
checks the rest.
