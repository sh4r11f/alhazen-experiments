Each trial starts with a word in a white rectangle: NEAR or FAR. Then a plain gray screen
with a small black target in the middle: look at its centre until the picture appears.

Then a picture appears: a photograph of a place, with things close to the camera, within
arm's reach, and things further away, across the room, the street or the field (in some
sessions the picture is a drawing of large shapes in front of a far surface instead). The
picture flickers: it keeps alternating with a slightly changed version of itself, with
brief gray flashes in between. Exactly one object is different between the two versions:
it appears and disappears, changes its colour, or moves. The change keeps repeating until
you see it.

The word tells you where in depth the change will most likely be: NEAR means one of the
things close to the camera, FAR means one of the things further away. It is usually right,
but not always, so keep looking everywhere if you do not find it.

Once the picture is on, look wherever you like. Press SPACE as soon as you see what is
changing.

Then two questions:

- Where: numbered boxes appear on the picture. Press the number (1 to 4) of the box the
  change was in.
- What kind: press the LEFT arrow if something appeared and disappeared, the DOWN arrow
  if it changed colour, the RIGHT arrow if it moved.

If you do not see the change within a minute, the trial ends by itself.

Press SPACE to begin.
