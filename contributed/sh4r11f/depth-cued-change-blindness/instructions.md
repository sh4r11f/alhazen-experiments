Each trial starts with a word in a white rectangle: NEAR or FAR. Then a plain gray screen.

Then a picture appears. It shows a layer of large, sharp shapes in front of a far
surface with smaller, paler shapes on it. The picture flickers: it keeps alternating with
a slightly changed version of itself, with brief gray flashes in between. Exactly one
shape is different between the two versions: it appears and disappears, changes its
colour, or moves. The change keeps repeating until you see it.

The word tells you where in depth the change will most likely be: NEAR means one of the
shapes in front, FAR means one of the shapes on the far surface. It is usually right, but
not always, so keep looking everywhere if you do not find it.

Keep your eyes wherever you like. Press SPACE as soon as you see what is changing.

Then two questions:

- Where: numbered boxes appear on the picture. Press the number (1 to 4) of the box the
  change was in.
- What kind: press the LEFT arrow if something appeared and disappeared, the DOWN arrow
  if it changed colour, the RIGHT arrow if it moved.

If you do not see the change within a minute, the trial ends by itself.

Press SPACE to begin.
